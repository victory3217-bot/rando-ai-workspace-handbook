# Learner workbook (learner-workbook)

> This workbook gathers the key exercise forms of CH01 to CH14 into one record document. It contains no new content: the forms are copied from the chapters, and the form numbers `[ ]` follow each chapter's numbers exactly. If you need an explanation, see the chapter. **This workbook has no answers.**
>
> **The times are unverified estimates.** The times below are the author's estimates and have never been tested with real learners. If you run short of time, fill in only some boxes of a form and fill in the rest afterward.
>
> **Finishing the workshop does not mean you have finished building your own work environment.** The goal inside the workshop is a *Level 1 draft of candidate A*. Completing it is homework after the workshop (see "Follow-up tasks" at the very end).
